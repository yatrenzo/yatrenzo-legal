Yatrenzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how Yatrenzo handles information when you use its mobile application, including travel discovery, trip bookings, social features, messaging, location sharing, and safety features.

For privacy questions or requests, contact 1bharatgo@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

The information processed depends on the features you use:

  • Account and profile information: Name, email address, phone number, authentication information, profile photographs, biography, age, gender, city, languages, travel interests, and preferences you provide.
  • Travel and booking information: Trips, itineraries, destinations, dates, memberships, seat requests, accommodation or transport allocations, expenses, and booking history.
  • Traveller details: Booking forms can collect each traveller's name, age, gender, government ID type and number, phone number, and emergency contact details. These details are provided to the relevant trip organizer to manage the booking and travel arrangements. Only provide another person's details when you are authorized to do so.
  • Content and communications: Messages, enquiries, posts, comments, reviews, photographs, videos, and other content you submit, together with related delivery, read, and interaction records.
  • Location information: Device coordinates and associated timestamps or accuracy information when you permit location access and use location-based features. Locations you choose to share in messages or SOS alerts are also processed.
  • Payment and payout information: Payment references, amounts, booking charges, refunds, wallet records, and payment status. Organizers setting up payouts provide legal name, PAN, contact details, bank account and beneficiary details, and IFSC information for payout processing. Payment credentials entered into the payment provider's checkout are handled by that provider.
  • Technical and security information: IP addresses, session and device information, notification tokens, consent choices, request logs, and error information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, personalize travel discovery, manage trips and bookings, connect travellers with organizers and guides, deliver messages and notifications, process payments and payouts, support expense tracking, provide location and safety features, respond to requests, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Location sharing and SOS alerts

Location access supports maps, nearby discovery, routing, live-location sharing, and SOS alerts. When live-location sharing is enabled, your name and current location may be visible to other signed-in Yatrenzo users on the map, including users outside your trip group. You can turn off location sharing in the app and control location permission in your device settings.

When you trigger an SOS alert, your name, location, and alert message may be shared with trip members, organizers, assigned guides, administrators, and your configured emergency contacts. If you enable the nearby audience option, nearby participating users may also receive the alert. An SOS location can remain in alert records and trip messages after the live alert ends.

Turning off future location access does not remove locations previously shared in messages or alerts.

4. When information is shared

  • With other users: Profile and social content are visible according to the feature and audience involved. Messages are visible to their recipients. Booking and traveller details are available to the relevant organizers. Location and SOS information are shared as described above.
  • With service providers: Providers supporting hosting, databases, media storage, transactional email, app updates, notification delivery, payments, maps, and routing process information needed for those functions.
  • For payments: Razorpay processes payment and organizer payout information where those features are available.
  • For communications: Expo and device notification services support push delivery. Brevo supports transactional email where configured.
  • For maps and routing: OpenStreetMap-based services and, where configured, Google mapping services may receive search queries, requested map areas, route coordinates, IP addresses, and related request information.
  • For legal and security purposes: Information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse, protect people, or enforce service terms.

We do not sell personal or sensitive user data. Information you disclose to another user or an independent service may also be subject to that recipient's handling practices.

5. Device permissions

Depending on the feature, the app may request location, camera, photo or media access, microphone access, and notification permission. These permissions support the corresponding features, such as sharing location, selecting or capturing media, audio features, and receiving updates. You can manage permissions in device settings. Denying permission may disable the related feature.

6. Data retention and security

Account information, bookings, content, communications, and related records are retained while needed to operate the service, manage transactions, address disputes, prevent abuse, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

The latest live-map position is updated as new positions arrive. The service is configured to periodically remove live-location records that have not been updated for more than 24 hours. This cleanup does not cover locations separately saved in messages, booking information, or SOS records.

We use security measures including authenticated access, protected password storage, and encrypted connections to production services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can update profile details and manage location and notification settings in the app. Under Profile → Account & Security, you can download your data or choose Delete my account. Account deletion requires identity confirmation and may require outstanding bookings, payments, or refunds to be resolved first.

You can also request account deletion, access, or correction without using the app by emailing 1bharatgo@gmail.com. For deletion, use the subject “Yatrenzo account deletion request” and identify the email address or phone number registered to your account. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or full government ID documents. We may ask you to confirm account ownership.

Deletion removes the account and associated records from active service databases. Some information may need to be retained for legal, payment, security, or dispute purposes. Copies already received by other users or held by independent payment providers are outside the account deletion process. Contact support about uploaded media or any information that remains after deletion. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

8. Children

Yatrenzo accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Adults providing information about accompanying travellers must have the authority and any necessary guardian permission to do so. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account or personal information has been provided without authorization.

9. Processing locations

Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Data protection rules can differ between countries. Contact us for questions about providers handling your information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel.

For questions, complaints, or privacy requests, contact Yatrenzo Support at 1bharatgo@gmail.com.
